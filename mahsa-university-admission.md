## Admission Requirements at MAHSA University

Admission requirements depend on the programme and level of study. Applicants may need to prepare the following documents:

- Academic certificates and transcripts
- A valid passport for international applications
- English language proficiency results, if required
- Completed application forms
- Passport-sized photographs, if requested
- Additional documents required for the selected programme

Students should confirm the exact requirements with the university's admissions team before submitting an application.

## Tuition Fees at MAHSA University

Tuition fees vary according to the selected course, programme duration, and level of study. Additional expenses may include accommodation, food, transportation, insurance, visa-related charges, and other living costs.

To prepare a realistic budget, students should:

1. Check the latest tuition fees for their preferred course.
2. Ask about registration and other additional charges.
3. Estimate accommodation and monthly living expenses.
4. Confirm applicable international student fees.
5. Ask whether scholarships or financial assistance are available.

It is advisable to obtain an official fee quotation before making financial decisions.

## How to Apply to MAHSA University

The admission process generally involves the following steps:

### Step 1: Choose Your Course

Research the programmes available and select one that matches your academic background and career interests.

### Step 2: Check the Entry Requirements

Review the academic qualifications, English language requirements, and any programme-specific conditions.

### Step 3: Prepare Your Documents

Gather your academic records, passport, photographs, and other documents requested by the university.

### Step 4: Submit Your Application

Apply through the university's official application channel or an authorised representative, where applicable.

### Step 5: Follow Up on Your Application

Monitor your application status and respond to requests for additional documents.

### Step 6: Complete the Required Formalities

After receiving an offer, follow the university's instructions regarding acceptance, payments, registration, and any applicable student visa procedures.
